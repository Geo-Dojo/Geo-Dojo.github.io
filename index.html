    How to play

    🎯 Risking your points: Dares

    Once you've played a couple of rounds, after each answer you can take a dare on the next question. Slide to put up to half the points you have on the line, before you see the next clue.

    • Name it right and those points come back to you as a bonus, as big as your dare but no bigger than what that question scores. Clues trim it. On the final question the bonus is doubled.
    • Name the wrong country and the points you dared are gone (your pin still earns its own points).
    • Leave it at No dare to play it safe. Only the country counts for a dare, not where your pin lands.

    Example: you have 400 points and dare half, so 200 are on the line. The next question scores 250: you get those 250 plus a 200 bonus. Name the wrong country and you lose the 200.

    How scoring works

    The basics

    • Country: 50 points for naming it. Pin: up to 50, more the closer it lands.
    • Perfect: the right country with your pin inside it earns a +10 bonus, multiplied like the rest. Perfects earn belts, from White to Black, and each belt wins a new map.
    • Streak: each right answer in a row multiplies the next one by 1.5, up to ×4. Two-choice rounds (higher or lower, north or south) are answered with one tap, no pin: they pay 40 for the name and count half a step.
    • Clues are optional. Each takes a percentage off that question's points. After Eliminate a wrong answer, Eliminate another clears a second one for a bigger cut.
    Everything else: levels, streak bonuses, surprises, power-ups

    More on points

    • The final question counts double. Trek pays ×1.25, Expedition ×1.5 and Uncharted ×2.
    • Find it: just the pin. Small countries count from 500 km away; big ones need a closer pin and pay a bit less.
    • Tricky facts that most players miss pay up to ×1.25.
    • Countries you miss come back in later rounds until you get them right.

    Hot streaks

    • Fever: five perfects in a row and your next three questions pay × 2.
    • Bonus round: 10 right in a row on Stroll or Trek (9 on Expedition, 8 on Uncharted) unlocks three timed bonus questions, after a 3-second countdown. Run out of time and you can continue once, for half your bonus points.
    • Encore: a perfect round, every pin inside the right country, earns one extra question with a gift-box multiplier.
    • Prize chest: play past the end of a round (a bonus round or an encore) and you pick one of three chests.

    Surprises

    • Mystery multiplier: hidden on random questions, at least once a round. After you lock one in, a mystery box opens and multiplies the points you've banked since the last one (× 1.1 to × 2), whether that answer scored or not.
    • Treasure country: one secret country a day pays × 5 if you name it. Surprise multipliers together top out at × 5.
    • Shared treasure: a pool for everyone playing today. It grows with every game, and the first perfect pin on the secret treasure country wins it all.
    • Golden stars: sometimes a golden star shoots across the globe. Catch it (tap it) before you lock in. Three in a round unlock the bonus round; in Survival, three earn an extra life.
    • Dares: sure of yourself? After each answer you can put up to half your points on the line for the next one: name it right and they come back as a bonus, name it wrong and they're gone.

    Power-ups and rewards

    • Power-ups: the ⚡ power meter on the globe fills with every right answer and faster with a perfect one. When it's full, you choose one of two power-ups: one that helps you find the answer (½ 50/50, 🔦 Spotlight, or ❄ Freeze in timed games) and one for your score (×2 Double up, 🛡 Shield, ↻ Second chance, or ♥ Extra life in Survival). Up to 4 choices a round (8 in Survival). Hold up to three; they last for the round. Everyone also gets a free ↻ Second chance each day.
    • Head start: rewards from outside a round (daily login, missions, collections, the weekly league, a prize chest) fill your power meter before the next round begins. At 100% you start with a choice ready.
    • Daily login: claim a power boost once a day along a 7-day row that builds to the biggest on day 7. Miss one day and it waits for you.
    • Passport sets: stamp every place on a continent to complete its set, for a badge and a full power meter.

    Levels and Survival

    • Levels: Stroll asks about well-known countries, takes its wrong choices from other continents, gives full pin points to any pin within 500 km and has no clock (a Perfect still needs the pin inside the border). Trek offers 6 choices, half from the same continent, about countries of 2 million people or more. Expedition has you type the name (small typos are fine) and leaves out tiny territories. Uncharted hides the borders, asks about every place, allows two clues per question and always has the clock. Pins are scored a little more strictly at each step.
    • Survival: endless questions and three lives. It warms up with capital cities, then climbs from Stroll through Trek and Expedition to Uncharted (with a life back at the last two), and the clock loses a second every four questions, from 25 down to 12. Every tenth question is a boss worth × 3.

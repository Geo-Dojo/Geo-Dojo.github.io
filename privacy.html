Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Geo Dojo is a geography game. The short version: it has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not collect personal information.

What the game saves

Your progress (passport stamps, the places you mark as Been there or Want to go, scores, streaks and settings) is saved on your own device, in your browser's storage or the app's. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the site's or app's data erases it. The Passport screen can make a backup code that you keep yourself.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

Other services

The game's files, including its fonts and maps, come from this site itself. Each fact links to the Wikipedia article it was checked against; following a link opens Wikipedia, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for, collect or share personal information from anyone, including children, and it has no chat, no purchases and no advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
